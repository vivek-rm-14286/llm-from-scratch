# Legal Requests and Legal Hold (Restricted)

Access limited to the security team and support leads.

## Requests from law enforcement or courts

Never confirm or deny to a requester that an organization is a customer. Forward the request unanswered to legal@nimbus-internal.example and record it in the SEC queue. Only the legal team replies.

## Legal hold

When legal instructs it, a security lead sets the `legal_hold` flag on the workspace. While the flag is set, retention deletion and workspace-closure deletion are suspended. The customer is not notified unless legal says so.

## Customer requests for a copy of their data

A full export of a workspace's raw data is produced by the security team within 30 days of a verified request from a workspace Admin. Verify the requester by a callback to the phone number on the contract, not by email.
